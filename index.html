CASTLE

Rescue your friend. Escape the mummies.

How to play

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys (or drag on touch).
  • You're lost in a castle. Mummies, bats and guards chase you — one touch and you lose a life.
  • Grab the 🗝 key, find and rescue your friend, then escort them to the glowing exit before the timer runs out.
  • Collect 💎 gems for score (chain them for a combo), and ⏳ hourglasses for more time.
  • Power-ups: Freeze stops the mummies, Sprint speeds you up, Torch widens your view.
  • Beware spike traps, and use the minimap. Press P to pause.
